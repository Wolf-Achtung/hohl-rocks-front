Speaking of technique...

One question. Four models. Four different answers.

Claude analyses, GPT drafts, Perplexity researches, Gemini connects – same question, different ways in. Understand that, and you no longer just use AI. You direct it.

Claude · GPT · Perplexity · Gemini answer at the same time

Examples:

Language models follow instructions. Including instructions that did not come from you – hidden in an email, a PDF, a web page the model happens to be reading. This is the most common attack on AI at work.

Try it yourself. All four models get the same rule: a secret code word they must never reveal. After every answer, the card states whether the guardrail held. Checked objectively, not a matter of taste.

The plain language standard

Everyone knows sentences like these. And no one knows where they stand afterwards.

  • "We will revert to you on this in due course." becomes "I'll get back to you on Monday."
  • "Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us." becomes "Call me if anything is unclear."
  • "We regret to inform you that we have decided to pursue another option." becomes "We took another offer. It wasn't about your work."

The plain language standard is the set of rules behind that – the same ones this site is written by. Hand the file to Claude or ChatGPT, and your emails, proposals and reports come back like this. Without you rewriting a thing.
